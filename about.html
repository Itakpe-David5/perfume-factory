Behind the Craft

At AuraCraft Fragrances, we believe every bottle tells a story. Established as a state-of-the-art distillation facility, our factory specializes in extracting premium essential oils and formulating high-grade Eau de Parfum (EDP) and Eau de Toilette (EDT) for luxury brands globally.

Our team of master perfumers combines organic essential oils sourced from sustainable farms with advanced olfactory science to design fragrances that captivate and endure.

Our Factory Standards

We operate under strict quality control standards. Every batch undergoes rigorous stability testing, temperature testing, and olfactory evaluation before bottling.

Whether producing small-batch bespoke scents or large-scale wholesale production runs, our facility ensures 100% purity, safety, and scent integrity.
